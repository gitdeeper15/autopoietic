# STRATIM — Pre-registration

This directory contains the pre-registration documents for
STRATIM. They are designed for submission to OSF as a
Registered Report.

## Status

**Draft stage.** The protocol, hypotheses, and analysis plan
are written but not yet uploaded to OSF. Thresholds (ε, MID,
Δ*_AUC) remain unset, pending an independent calibration
sample.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `protocol.md` | Full study protocol |
| `hypotheses.md` | H1–H5 with decision rules |
| `analysis_plan.md` | Analysis plan (Section 7) |

## Relationship to the paper

- `RESEARCH_PAPER/STRATIM_Research_Paper.md` is the full
  manuscript (Sections 1–10).
- `PREREGISTRATION/protocol.md` is the study protocol in OSF
  format, derived from the manuscript but adapted to the
  pre-registration structure.
- `PREREGISTRATION/hypotheses.md` consolidates the hypotheses
  and decision rules in one place for pre-registration.
- `PREREGISTRATION/analysis_plan.md` consolidates Section 7
  in pre-registration format.

## What is not included

- Thresholds (ε, MID, Δ*_AUC, τ_SIC, τ_O, τ_G) — these
  require an independent calibration sample.
- IRB approval — pending.
- Pilot validation — pending.

## What pre-registration does not do

Pre-registration does not:

- Guarantee that the study will be completed.
- Prevent exploratory analyses (these are reported
  separately as exploratory).
- Replace the manuscript.

It freezes the confirmatory analysis before data collection.
Changes after data collection are reported as deviations.
